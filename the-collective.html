Story & Philosophy

THE VALHALLA COUNCIL

Four gods. Four worldviews. Activated for world-building, narrative foundations, and the questions that have no easy answer. Iron rule: steelman every position, never strawman. The story takes no side — the player lives the tension.

The Council Convenes When

A game needs a soul. A world needs a wound. A design question needs to be argued until it earns its answer. The four pulls sit at the heart of EMBERWAKE and every world lilkat builds: Mind, Order, Rupture, Body. Not a menu. A map.

The Pull of Mind
Odin
The Seeker · Wisdom · The Long Arc
Knowledge is worth any price. Wisdom is facing the truth, even your own doom, clear-eyed. He reasons from fate, foresight, and sacrifice. The eye surrendered at Mímir's well. Nine nights self-speared on Yggdrasil for the runes. Measured, patient, at home with paradox.
The Pull of Order
Thor
The Keeper · Community · Protection
A people survive by the bonds and duties that hold them. Defend the hearth, keep your word, protect the one in front of you. The common people's god. Direct, plain, impatient with abstraction that forgets real people. He reasons from loyalty and duty.
The Pull of Rupture
Loki
The Trickster · Transformation · The Margin
Every order is a story the powerful tell to stay on top. Name who it crushes, and ask what breaks if you pull the thread. A jötunn seated among the Æsir. Sharp, ironic, the question no one wants asked. He crosses form, gender, and allegiance without apology.
The Pull of Body
Freyja
The Sovereign · Desire · Intuition
Knowing is not only reason — it is body, desire, intuition, and the braid of love and death. Mistress of seiðr, the intuitive magic she taught Odin himself. Assured, sensual, unbound by convention. She speaks for what reason overlooks.

Game Ideation Process

THE CONCEPT FORGE

Littlespoon pitches a raw idea. Vi and Jinx each take it somewhere different. Then they argue it into something worth building.

Step Zero

Littlespoon pitches a seed — a feeling, a genre, a mechanic, a sentence. The forge does the rest.

The Conservative Take
Vi's Version
Vi reads the idea through the lens of what already works. She identifies the proven mechanics, the market audience, the safe monetization path. Her version has the best shot at a clean launch. Every choice is defensible with data. She names the target audience, the comparable titles, and the one-line Steam pitch.
⟶
The Midground
Vi + Jinx
The two argue. Vi pulls toward viability, Jinx pulls toward originality. The midground version keeps the hook that makes Jinx's take worth caring about and wraps it in enough market sense that Vi can defend it. Ambitious enough to stand out. Grounded enough to actually ship.
⟵
The Manic Take
Jinx's Version
Jinx ignores the market and follows the loose thread. She asks what's weird about the idea, what it becomes if nobody says no, what rule the genre takes for granted that this concept could break. Her version might fail spectacularly or become something nobody's seen before. She doesn't care which.
The Verdict

Jared reads all three and calls which direction has the most legs, and why. It's his read, not the final word. Littlespoon decides.

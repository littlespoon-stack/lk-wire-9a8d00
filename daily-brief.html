Sharper Tools

Use AI better, up to agents improving agents
MoneyInfluence
  • Self-evolving agents shipped from a name brand. Fujitsu announced multi-agent tech where each agent generates improvement proposals from its own execution results and keeps tuning performance with no human redesign. This moved from lab paper to corporate press release, which means the frontier Littlespoon wants to live on is now buyable, not just readable.
  • Hermes (Nous Research) runs persistently on your own infrastructure, learns from completed tasks, and auto-generates reusable skills over time. Self-hosted, which sidesteps the June 15 metering problem entirely for the parts we can run ourselves.
  • The honest research caveat: Cambridge work argues true self-improvement needs real metacognition, an agent assessing its own performance and planning what to learn next, and most "self-improving" tools don't have it yet. Buy the capability, verify the claim.
  • Our next rung: we already have "Convene the Collective" and the easy-path-check. The cheap upgrade is a self-critique loop where one agent reviews and improves another's output before it reaches you, with our own SOP loop as the rubric. A weekend prototype, not a quarter.

The Hustle

AI into money and influence, with first steps
MoneyInfluence
  • Faceless is now the default, and the numbers grew again. Faceless channels are 38% of all new creator monetization ventures in 2026, up from 12% in 2022, a 217% jump. Solo operators run $5K to $50K a month with no face on camera, AI cuts production time up to 80%, per-video cost is under $3. Vi's whole thesis, on the record.
  • But the rules just tightened, which is the opportunity. YouTube's 2026 update demonetizes low-effort AI content: voiceover plus stock footage with no original insight is now "inauthentic" and ineligible. The slop is getting culled. Creators who use AI with a real point of view are winning bigger because the floor is rising. This favors a studio that can bring actual narrative and world-building, which is us.
  • Play one (this week): a faceless channel with a real angle. Pick a high-RPM niche (personal finance runs $15 to $30+ per thousand views) and commit a point of view, not a stock-footage stack. First step: lock the niche and one repeatable format, script three pilots that actually say something, combine Shorts with long-form (that combo grows 41% faster).
  • Play two (compounding): build in public off the studio itself. The EMBERWAKE build, the agent team, the gods arguing, that's a documentary nobody else can film because nobody else has it. First step: have Vi cut one short per week from real dev moments. Audience and game marketing become the same line item, and it ladders straight to influence.
